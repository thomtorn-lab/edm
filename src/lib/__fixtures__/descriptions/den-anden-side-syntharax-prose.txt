Syntharax marks its third anniversary with a night at Den Anden Side, a trance and progressive trance edition presented under the club's Normal Selection door policy.

Headlining is London-based DJ Sybil [she/her], whose psychedelic, genre-fluid sets blend deep techno, breaks and trance; she has presented her sound at Dekmantel, Waking Life, Freerotation, Berlin Atonal and De School. Berlin-based producer and live act Psicotek [he/him] joins, alongside local talents peachlyfe [they/she], FILTH.y [she/her] and Dico Nemus [he/him]. Syntharax residents Floes [he/him], F2B [he/him] and CF682 [they/them] complete the lineup.

Admission is 160 DKK in presale, with limited presale tickets available through Resident Advisor, and 170 DKK at the door. Entry is two for one before 00:30, and 80 DKK after 05:30. Re-entry is possible all night, and entrance closes one hour before the club, earliest at 7 am. Attendees must be 20 or older. Lockers are free to use with a lock, which can be brought along or bought at the entrance for 50 DKK.

On Normal Selection nights, door staff curate the crowd with an increased focus on the vibe and balance of the audience, and expect anyone entering to have a basic knowledge of the club's policy.
