Rooted in Berlin, Britta Arnold channels the city’s boundless creativity and ever-evolving energy into her music, which reflects her surroundings—diverse, grounded, and always forward-thinking. Britta's musical journey began in 2006 when she co-founded the iconic Berlin label Bar25. Since then, she’s built an impressive career, playing at renowned clubs and festivals across Europe, America, and Asia. As a DJ and producer, she has captured the imagination of a global audience with her hypnotic beats and deep groovy rhythms. Whether in the studio crafting new material or performing late into the night, Britta is constantly exploring new sounds and pushing the limits of her musical expression. Her recent work has seen her release new tracks on the Kiosk ID, MiZi MuZiK and Around Midnight labels, marking a fresh chapter in her sonic exploration. Collaborations with Mira, Sascha Cawa and Tatyana have brought an exciting new dynamic to her sound, pushing boundaries and further expanding her already rich discography. These projects have injected her music with new energy, capturing the attention of both longtime fans and new listeners. In addition to her latest releases, Britta has an extensive back catalog of music, including releases on well-established labels like Katermukke, Kindish, Harthouse and Heinz. Over the years she has remixed tracks from distinguished artists such as Satori and Stavroz, while her own music has been reinterpreted by legends like Claude VonStroke, Marco Ressman and Ruede Hagelstein. Beyond her music, Britta has also created a lasting impact on Berlin's nightlife with her popular party series, “GRRR Mit BRRR.” The event, now a staple at Kater Blau, attracts a loyal following and has become a celebrated part of the city’s electronic culture.
