FAQS

After reading this section, if you still have any questions, please feel free to contact us.

  • ENTRY POLICY

    Security reserves the right to refuse or restrict entry where necessary to ensure the safety, order, or overall quality of the event. This includes situations where an attendee’s appearance or behavior may reasonably be considered inappropriate for the event setting. In such cases, admission may be denied without refund.

  • PHOTOGRAPHY AND VIDEO CONSENT

    By attending the events, you agree that photos and video recordings may be taken and used by the organizer for promotional and marketing purposes, including publication online and on social media.

  • PROFESSIONAL CAMERAS POLICY

    During the event, professional cameras, action cameras (e.g., GoPro), and other filming equipment are not allowed. Please use only personal devices (such as mobile phones).

  • CAN I ENTER THE EVENT AT ANY TIME?

    You must arrive before the entry time indicated on your ticket. If you arrive later, a late fee of DKK80 applies. However, if you have a ticket type marked 'Entry Any Time', you can enter freely without paying the late fee.

  • CAN I LEAVE THE EVENT AND COME BACK LATER?

    A “NO RE-ENTRY” policy applies at the event. If you leave the event and wish to return, a new ticket must be purchased.

  • WILL THERE BE FOOD AVAILABLE AT THE EVENT?

    Yes. Food trucks will be available throughout the event, offering meals and snacks for purchase.

  • WILL TICKETS BE AVAILABLE AT THE DOOR?

    Yes, tickets may be available at the door, but this is subject to availability and tickets are more expensive. Please note that during sold-out events, tickets at the door are sold on a “1 out, 1 in” basis.

  • IS THERE A WARDROBE SERVICE?

    Yes, there is a wardrobe service available for a small fee. You can securely store your belongings and pick them up when you leave the event.

  • HOW CAN I PAY AT THE EVENT?

    We only accept card payments at the event.

  • IS THERE A DRESS CODE?

    To maintain the atmosphere and style of the event, we kindly ask you to consider the event’s theme and avoid sportswear (e.g., tracksuits, workout clothes).

  • CAN I SMOKE ON THE PARTY GROUNDS?

    Smoking is only allowed outside of the venue. Please be considerate of others and dispose of cigarette butts properly.

  • EVENT RULES

    According to Danish law, the possession or use of any illegal narcotic substances is strictly prohibited. Individuals found using drugs will be immediately removed from the event. Thank you for helping us ensure that the event is safe and enjoyable for everyone.
