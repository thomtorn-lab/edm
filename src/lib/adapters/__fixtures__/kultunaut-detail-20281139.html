Ecstatic dance by Range of Motion

Ecstatic Dance by Range Of MotionOctober Theme: TBA. (formerly known as Ecstatic Dance with Grundstoff & Ionstrom).

 In the framework of our new association, Range of Motion, our dance evenings are evolving into a bit more intentionally held and themed format. The events will open with a session of guided movement exploration, inspired by the theme of the night, before the dance unfolds into a free ecstatic wave. You can participate in the parts of the program that suits you.
We encourage all participants to become members, for a continued fellowship and discounted tickets on all events, which replaces our previous early bird-system.
WHAT IS ECSTATIC DANCE?
Our invitation is to approach dance as a moving meditation, as a way to sweat your prayers and celebrate being alive, with all that it includes!
Have you ever been longing for a dancefloor where you can set yourself free to dance as if no one is watching, and where everyone else is doing the same? A dancefloor with a high and clear vibe - no alcohol, no small talk, no smartphones, no shoes. Simply a group of people moving freely, for the pure joy of it, the therapy, the medicine, the pleasure.
Whatever mood you might be in when you arrive, let it move. There is no agenda and nothing you can do wrong. The dancefloor is a potential self-healing place, a potential meditation in motion, a potential ground for meeting others in movement - or an opportunity to move to really juicy music. Come and let your body lead the way!

THE MUSIC
The DJ's intention is to create a smooth wave with great variety - a multicolor journey with room for many aspects of being human.
We always start out soft and gentle, build intensity, and land back into gentle at the end.
Expect electronic depth blended with ethnic and organic elements - voices, percussion, spacious bass, raw rhythms. Music that supports you to experience yourself and your moving body in a wide variety of ways.
Sometimes live elements will be woven into the journey as Range of Motion opens up for new collaborations with musicians and facilitators aligned with the theme of the evening.

CEREMONIAL CACAO
We offer the option to drink Ceremonial Cacao as a supplement to the dance. Make sure to pre-order cacao latest 18.30 the day before - see above!
Ceremonial Cacao can enhance your dance, energize the body and connect you more deeply with yourself and others. It works with your natural energy - supporting you to soften and open, while staying grounded.
Our cacao will often be lightly infused with spices or herbs - information is given where you buy the cacao ticket.
Bring your own cup - let's save single-use materials.

ECSTATIC DANCE GUIDELINES

We dance barefoot.
We dance without talking - sounds are most welcome
We get high on the same vibes - no alcohol or other mind-altering substances.
We dance off screen - no mobile phones, photo or filming on the dancefloor.
We look for consent before touching others.

Take responsibility for your own yes/no and respect the (verbal or non-verbal) no of others. Own your experience and help create the best possible evening for all.
No ticket refunds. You can resell your ticket via the fb event page and transfer it through Billetto. Please be aware of fake fb profiles pretending to sell tickets.
