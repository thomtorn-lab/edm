The concept

An OPERA is not a RAVE.

Gravity Opera is our melodic techno series - built like theatre. Months of planning and days of building, for eight hours that only exist once. The room, the lights and the live performers are designed for that night, then taken apart.

Whatever this chapter looks like, it won't look like that again. Nobody gets to see it twice.

Before you buy

The questions that
actually block a purchase.

Can I enter the event at any time?

You must arrive before the entry time indicated on your ticket. If you arrive later, a late fee of DKK 80 applies. However, if you have a ticket type marked 'Entry Any Time', you can enter freely without paying the late fee.

Can I leave the event and come back later?

A “no re-entry” policy applies at the event. If you leave and wish to return, a new ticket must be purchased.

Will tickets be available at the door?

Yes, tickets may be available at the door, but this is subject to availability and tickets are more expensive. During sold-out events, door tickets are sold on a “1 out, 1 in” basis.

Will there be food available at the event?

Yes. Food trucks are available throughout the event, offering meals and snacks for purchase.

Is there a wardrobe service?

Yes, there is a wardrobe service available for a small fee. You can securely store your belongings and pick them up when you leave.

How can I pay at the event?

We only accept card payments at the event.

Is there a dress code?

There is no strict dress code — come as you are and express yourself. We encourage creative, comfortable, rave-style outfits. Just make sure your clothing is appropriate for a party environment and safe to dance in.

Can I smoke on the party grounds?

Smoking is only allowed outside the venue. Please be considerate of others and dispose of cigarette butts properly.
