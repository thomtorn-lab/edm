Ca7riel y Paco Amoroso

Friday 25 September

This concert has been moved to Poolen from K.B. Hallen. Tickets purchased for K.B. Hallen will remain valid for Poolen.

CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso follow the release of their new album FREE SPIRITS with the announcement of the FREE SPIRITS World Tour, an extensive tour that will bring the Argentine duo to Poolen on 25 September.

On FREE SPIRITS, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso expand their already unpredictable blend of trap, rock, pop and experimental elements into something bigger, sharper and more emotionally open without losing the humour, volatility and musicianship that have made them one of Latin America’s most captivating acts.

Please note:

The CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso concert on 25 September 2026 has been moved from K.B. Hallen to Poolen, Refshalevej 189, 1432 Copenhagen. Your tickets will remain valid for the new venue, and all timings remain unchanged: Doors at 18:30 and show at 20:00. If you originally purchased a seated ticket, your ticket will be valid for standing admission, as Poolen is a standing-only venue. The price difference between your original seated ticket and a standing ticket will be automatically refunded to you through Billetlugen.
