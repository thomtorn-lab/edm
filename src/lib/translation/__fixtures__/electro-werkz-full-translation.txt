Cold Connection-
Cold Connection is a Swedish synthpop/electropop band, formed in 2011 and consisting of Pontus Olsson (music) and Daniel Billqvist (vocals and lyrics).
They create atmospheric and energetic electronic pop music inspired by names such as Depeche Mode, Rational Youth, Jean Michel Jarre and Elegant Machinery, combined with a timeless sound. They have been described as a melancholic Erasure.
Their debut album Seconds Of Solitude from 2021 was very well received, and they continue to create catchy melodies. This live concert will be their long-awaited comeback on stage - and perhaps they will even play something completely new for you!
ee:man-
ee:man is a dark synth project that emerged in 2021 with the debut album Echoes of Life. The project combines dark synth, synthpop, EBM and industrial influences, creating atmospheric soundscapes driven by melancholic melodies, powerful rhythms and cinematic production.
ee:man is often compared to Recoil (Alan Wilder from Depeche Mode) because of the depth and layered approach to the music. Over time, the project has evolved towards a heavier and more rhythm-focused sound, while the dark and immersive identity has been preserved.
Recent releases have drawn comparisons with artists such as Nine Inch Nails and Front Line Assembly, placing ee:man at the crossroads between dark electronic music and industrial sound art.
This concert will be a new beginning for ee:man. He will not be alone on stage... wait and see
