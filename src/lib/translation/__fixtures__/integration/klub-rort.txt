WHAT: AFRO, SOUL, FUNK, DEEP HOUSE & MUCH MOREWHERE: Rört, Thoravej 35WHEN: Friday Evening SEPT 25th. Doors open 19:00, music starts & doors close 19:30. The event ends around 23:00.

KLUB RÖRT is a dancing praise to connection, wildness, stillness and exploration. It is your Friday evening conscious and curious dance floor where people get high on own supply - no drugs and no alcohol, just sweat and movement - and connect deeply to themselves and to others.
THE EVENING GRAVITATES to a lively and heartful dance floor, but will also create spaces for arriving, for chilling in between and for landing afterward as the dancefloor ends with a Gong-shower & Sound-journey. The intention is to give you a full Friday evening and send you of in a state where you can rest deeply and be ready to enjoy the rest of your weekend from a vital state.
IT’S NOT WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE it is what it feels like and in KLUB RÖRT we seek to share that attitude of body positivity. We seek to create a safe(r) space and a space where you feel supported by the vibe to really dig deep into yourself as you lean into the music.
ABOUT THE DJ:David Ramati is Danish dj who has played the scene in Denmark for 2 decades as well as internationally renowned, having played everything from Burning man in Nevada to The carnival at Copacabana in Brazil.Dj David Ramati has a special love for All kinds of music with a deep Groove and is known to keep a dancefloor going till the break of dawn.
SOUND JOURNEY - with Mollie MaunaWe start of the Klub Rört dancefloor landing together in our bodies with a Sound journey - and we round of the dance floor in the same way - all facilitated by Mollie Maluna.
