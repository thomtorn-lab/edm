A massive celebration of R&B from the 90’s and 00’s awaits August 2027 at Poolen’s new outdoor area Outside.

Get ready for 7 hours of non-stop dancing to crisp R&B tunes from the ’90s and 2000s on August 15, when UK party collective R&B Lovers takes over Poolen. It’ll be a tribute to two iconic decades when R&B flourished and topped charts around the world – and R&B Lovers’ DJs are ready to deliver the very best tracks from the era.

Outside

Outside is the open-air extension of Poolen, going beyond the club room, expanding our warehouse ethos under the sky. Outside is our summer domain, built for large-scale parties set between water, concrete and air at Refshaleøen, just outside Poolen.

Spontaneous and alive by design, Outside is a flexible outdoor space, merging a high capacity dancefloor with cozy nooks, secluded corners and hideaways amidst lush greenery and artistic lighting for that playful balance of hanging out as much as dancing – from late night to first light.

Across the summer season, Outside will host a spectrum of events, ranging from Detroit legend Omar S, underground artists on the rise, festival afterparties and genre-spanning club concepts. From sunlit sessions to peak-time nocturnal sets, Outside allows for scale, variation and experimentation.
